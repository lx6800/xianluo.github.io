Introduction

About Me

Hello! I am Xian Luo (罗贤), affiliated with the School of Information Engineering at Peking University. I am interested in the intersection of multimodal learning, large language models, and efficient AI systems.

I am particularly interested in how vision-language models understand visual information, reason over multimodal evidence, and generate reliable responses efficiently. I am also interested in inference algorithms and system-level optimizations that make large models faster and more practical.

This homepage introduces my research interests and ongoing exploration. I will update it with publications, projects, and other academic activities as they become available.

Research Interests

What I Am Interested In

01 / MULTIMODAL AI

Multimodal Large Language Models

Vision-language models, multimodal representation learning, cross-modal alignment, visual instruction following, and unified multimodal understanding.

02 / EFFICIENT INFERENCE

LLM Inference Acceleration

Speculative decoding, parallel token generation, draft-and-verify algorithms, adaptive computation, and latency reduction for autoregressive generation.

03 / VISUAL REASONING

Vision-Language Reasoning

Visual evidence selection, fine-grained image understanding, temporal reasoning in video, and relationships between visual observations and natural-language questions.

04 / AI SYSTEMS

AI Infrastructure and Model Optimization

Efficient attention computation, KV-cache optimization, memory-efficient inference, GPU kernels, and hardware-aware optimization for large models.

05 / TRAINING

Efficient Training and Adaptation

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning, knowledge distillation, preference and process supervision, and training strategies for improving model efficiency.

06 / RELIABLE AI

Reliable Multimodal Generation

Faithful visual grounding, hallucination reduction, output consistency, and balancing generation quality with inference efficiency.

Research Directions

Topics I Would Like to Explore

1. Speculative Decoding for Vision-Language Models

Investigating how visual information can guide draft-token generation, improve draft acceptance, and accelerate multimodal autoregressive decoding while preserving target-model generation behavior.

2. Question-Aware Visual Evidence Selection

Exploring methods that identify question-relevant visual evidence and model relationships among visual features, with the goal of improving multimodal reasoning and reducing unnecessary computation.

3. Efficient Video-Language Understanding

Exploring efficient processing of long videos, temporal information selection, event relationships, and multimodal inference strategies for video question answering.

4. Hardware-Aware LLM Optimization

Studying the interaction between inference algorithms, GPU execution, memory movement, attention computation, and practical end-to-end latency.

5. Training and Inference Co-Design

Exploring how lightweight adaptation, distillation, and inference-time verification can work together to improve the efficiency and reliability of large models.
